/ Process

How this works
day to day.

4 stages. Each ends in a thing you can approve, request changes on, or kill.

  1. / Step 01

    Discovery & ICP

    Workshop your ICP, value props, and current funnel. Audit existing campaigns and identify the fastest wins.

  2. / Step 02

    Foundations

    Build landing pages, capture forms, scoring rules, nurture sequences. Wire tracking end-to-end.

  3. / Step 03

    Activate

    Launch multi-channel campaigns. Monitor daily, optimise weekly. Hot leads start flowing within 30 days.

  4. / Step 04

    Optimise

    Monthly review: what's working, what's dead. Cycle budget into winners, kill the losers, ship new tests.
