/ Process

How this works
day to day.

4 stages. Each ends in a thing you can approve, request changes on, or kill.

  1. / Step 01

    Brief & moodboard

    We listen, we scout references, we send back a deck. You approve before the camera comes out.

  2. / Step 02

    Pre-production

    Location lock, talent confirmed, schedule, equipment list, contingency plan for weather or chaos.

  3. / Step 03

    Shoot day

    We run set. You show up if you want. Real-time review on a tethered monitor for the moments that matter.

  4. / Step 04

    Edit & deliver

    Selects within 48 hours. Final retouch within 10 working days. Revisions baked in.
