/ Deliverables

What you actually get.

No vague ‘strategy’ line items. Every box is a thing that lands in your inbox or your repo.

  • Pre-production

    Treatment, script, storyboard, location scout, talent and crew booking. We over-prepare so set runs smooth.

  • Cinema cameras

    Sony FX-series cinema rigs, gimbals, drones, sliders. We bring whatever the shot demands.

  • Sound

    Lavs, booms, ambient capture. Pro Tools mix. Music licensed or original score commissioned.

  • 4K HDR delivery

    Spec'd for web, broadcast, OOH, and cinema. We deliver the file and the source if you want it.

/ Process

How this works
day to day.

4 stages. Each ends in a thing you can approve, request changes on, or kill.

  1. / Step 01

    Concept & treatment

    We write the film. Tone, pacing, visual references, music direction — captured in a treatment deck you sign off.

  2. / Step 02

    Pre-production

    Casting, locations, crew, schedule, gear. The shoot starts in the spreadsheet.

  3. / Step 03

    Production

    We run set. Tethered monitor for live review. You direct as much or as little as you want.

  4. / Step 04

    Post-production

    Edit, sound design, color grade, mix. Two rounds of revisions baked in. Final master in 3 weeks.
